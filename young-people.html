For the screen-born generation

You were handed a phone before you were handed roots.

If you've ever felt like you're the most connected generation in history — and somehow the loneliest — this page is for you. Not a lecture. Not a "you should delete all your apps" guilt trip. Just eight things nobody told you that you actually need.

Honestly, does any of this sound familiar?

  • You can scroll for three hours straight but can't sit in silence for three minutes.
  • You have hundreds of followers but you're not sure who'd actually show up if you needed them.
  • You've watched a million tutorials but you're not sure you could cook a proper meal, fix anything, or manage a budget.
  • Your phone tracks you hundreds of times a day — and you know it, but you're not sure what to do about it.
  • You've absorbed everyone else's opinions about what matters — but you're not sure what you actually believe.
  • You're exhausted by the pressure to be visible, successful, and happy — all the time, online.

This isn't your fault. You were born into a world that was already optimised for something other than your flourishing. The algorithms, the notifications, the pressure to perform — none of it was designed with your good in mind. But here's the thing: you can build something different. And you don't have to do it alone.

That's what one to eight is. A guide, an app, and a community — built for people who want more than what the feed is selling them.
