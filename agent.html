The onetoeight agent

An assistant that teaches the eight, and runs on your machine.

OneToEight Agent is a personal AI assistant you install on your own computer. It walks you through the eight essentials of the guide, one small thing a day, and teaches you to run AI of your own along the way.

Get the agent Read the guide first

What it is

It is an assistant. Not a companion. It makes you more productive and more creative, and gives time back for real life: real skills, real people, real places.

It does not lecture, and it does not nag. It draws the wisdom out of you with questions, holds the rhythm gently, and restarts you without scolding when you fall off.

The eight essentials are best learned by doing one small thing a day, not by reading a book. The agent exists to make that rhythm stick.

The eight essentials

01Silence · sit still, and be alone with your own thoughts
02Body · sleep, food, water, movement, sunlight
03Skills · useful real-world competence: cook, fix, build, budget
04Digital Self-Defence · privacy, attention, identity literacy
05Real Community · people who know your name
06Knowledge Without Wi-Fi · owned, not rented
07Nature · prescribed time outside, non-negotiable
08Tested Faith · yours, and it holds under pressure

Two of the most important requirements for the future.

There is no better way to learn AI skills in a soft way than while learning the eight essentials. The two run side by side, one small thing a day.

Stay human.

The eight keep the human things human: silence without a screen, a body that works, skills in your hands, people who know your name, knowledge that stays when the Wi-Fi drops, time outside, faith that holds under pressure. No agent can do these for you, and this one never pretends to.

Use AI constructively.

You install your own agent, shape it, and run it every day. Setting it up teaches you what these systems are. Living with one teaches judgement: when to reach for the machine, when to think alone, and how to keep the ideas your own. It works for you, never instead of you.

No hype. No fear. Just daily practice.

Local-first, like the guide

  • Runs on your own computer. Hermes is free and open source, and the agent profile is yours to shape.
  • Learn AI by running AI. You will set it up, direct it, and grow it: real agent skills, learned by doing.
  • Build personal software. For your own goals and use cases, or for the people around you: friends, family, and your local community. Whatever you make runs on your machine and stays yours.

Getting set up

You do not need to be technical. Seven short modules take you from a blank computer to a working agent, starting with the reasons behind it and finishing with you writing your own first skill.

Bring a laptop (Windows, Mac or Linux) and around an hour. Hermes is free; the modules walk you through connecting a model account as well.
